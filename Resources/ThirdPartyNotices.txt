The Swift application invokes wimlib-imagex as a separate command-line process.
It does not link to libwim. The CLI itself statically includes libwim and is
built without libntfs-3g or FUSE. Only macOS system libraries are required.

The matching wimlib-1.14.5-corresponding-source.tar.gz is distributed alongside
Win11-USB-Creator.zip. It contains the original upstream source release,
including its generated configure script, the scripts used to build the
bundled executable, licensing notices, and rebuilding instructions. Anyone
redistributing the app must also provide the corresponding source under the
